Where generative AI is used

The reconciliation engine remains deterministic and authoritative. On a completed run, you can explicitly request Gemini to investigate bounded unmatched, mismatched, duplicate, and uncertain records or ask a question about that run. Gemini receives only run-scoped transaction IDs, dates, amounts in cents, short descriptions, and deterministic statuses; source references and account details are excluded. AI explanations are hypotheses, citations are checked against saved records, and every suggestion requires human review. No AI call occurs unless you request it.
